ARTICLE 1 - COFFEE SHOPS CRISIS

BANYUWANGI'S COFFEE SHOPS FEEL THE HEAT AS BEAN PRICES SOAR. Banyuwangi, known for its robusta and arabica plantations in the Ijen area, is facing an economic paradox. While global coffee prices reached a record high due to crop failure in Vietnam and Brazil, local SMEs in Wongsorejo reported a decline in net profit. The Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) increased by 35%. The latest Income Statement shows gross revenue increased from 45 million to 52 million, but gross profit margin dropped from 55% to 38%. ARTICLE 2 - GO-PANGAN LOSS

The loss was driven by non-cash expenses, including depreciation expense for delivery trucks and bad debt expense due to farmers failing to repay micro-loans. On the liabilities side, the company has significant long-term debt and short-term bank loans. Interest expense reached 40 billion. Current ratio dropped below 1.0, indicating a solvency issue. The company uses accrual basis, not cash basis. Receivables were recorded as revenue before cash was received. Auditors are examining impairment of intangible assets like software. Investors await the statement of retained earnings and equity.
